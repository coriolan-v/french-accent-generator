# French accent style reference

`french23.mp3` and `french8.mp3` are the **french23** and **french8**
recordings from the Speech Accent Archive, George Mason University, by Steven
H. Weinberger and Matthew C. Kelley.

- Sources: https://accent.gmu.edu/samples/french23/ and
  https://accent.gmu.edu/samples/french8/
- Native language: French
- Birthplace: Ghent, Belgium
- Selection note: both published IPA transcriptions repeatedly mark uvular
  French `r` sounds (`[ʁ]` for french23 and `[ʀ]` for french8).
- License: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0
- License text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/

It is used only as a style reference for non-commercial local prototyping.
Replace it with an appropriately licensed or commissioned recording before any
commercial exhibition.
