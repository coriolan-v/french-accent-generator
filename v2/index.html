5 · Pyramid lights

"LED numbers" lights the first LED green and every 10th red. Count from the green LED along the strip to each corner, then list the sides in that order with blue, white, red or off, e.g. "27 off, 25 blue, 26 white, 25 red". Shift moves the whole flag further along the strip, wrapping around the base; a negative shift goes the other way. The buttons need the round display open with the lights connected.
